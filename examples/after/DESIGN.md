# Margin: design decisions

The direction slop-check wrote before fixing `../before/index.html`.

- **Product**: newsletter analytics for independent writers.
- **Character**: calm, literate, exact.
- **Surface**: app screen, worked in every day.
- **Bright element**: this week's bars in the growth chart, in ink blue, with the best day called out. Everything else stays in the neutrals.
- **Signature**: issue numbers (`No. 42`) in tabular figures, wherever an issue appears.

## Values

- Neutrals: page `#f4f4f2`, panel `#ffffff`, raised `#ececea`; text `#1b1b1f`, `#55565c`, `#6a6b72`; hairlines at 8 and 14 % of the text colour; field outlines `#85858b` (3:1).
- Accent: ink blue `#2d4fb3`, for the primary action, the current page and this week's data. Positive changes `#1d7a46`.
- Type: Avenir Next (system fallbacks), 12 to 28 px, weights 400 to 600, tabular figures for every number.
- Spacing on a 4 px grid; radius 4 to 14 by size; one ease-out curve, 150 ms on hover.
- Copy: the period as the page title, changes stated against a baseline, no exclamation marks, no badges.
